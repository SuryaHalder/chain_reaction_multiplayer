How to Play & Rules

🎯 Objective

Chain Reaction is a deterministic strategy game. Players take turns placing orbs into grid cells. Your goal is to eliminate all opponents' orbs and conquer the entire board!

💥 Critical Mass Thresholds

Each cell has a limit on how many orbs it can hold before it explodes into adjacent orthogonal cells:

2
CORNER CELLS
Explodes on 2nd orb
Splits in 2 directions
3
BORDER CELLS
Explodes on 3rd orb
Splits in 3 directions
4
CENTER CELLS
Explodes on 4th orb
Splits in 4 directions

⚔️ Capturing & Eliminations

  • Orb Capturing: When your exploding orbs land in a cell containing an opponent's orbs, you instantly take over all orbs in that cell!
  • Chain Cascade: If the newly captured cell exceeds critical mass, it explodes in your color too, igniting a cascading chain reaction!
  • First Round Immunity: In Round 1, no one can be eliminated until they have placed their first orb.
  • Player Elimination: After your first move, if you ever have 0 orbs remaining on the board, you are eliminated!
  • Sole Survivor Victory: The last remaining player on the board wins the match!
